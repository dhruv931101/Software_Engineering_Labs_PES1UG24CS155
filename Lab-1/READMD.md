## 1. Overview

This lab focuses on identifying and documenting functional and nonfunctional requirements and translating them into a UML use-case diagram and a use-case flow.

## 2. Project Scenario

**Warehouse Inventory & Pallet Location Tracker**

**Problem Statement:** #27

The system focuses on warehouse inventory tracking and pallet location management, including pallet scanning, shelf assignment, weight validation, stock movement logging, and inventory reporting.

## 3. Objectives

- Identify and document functional requirements (FRs) and nonfunctional requirements (NFRs).
- Define priorities and measurable acceptance criteria.
- Model system functionality using a UML use-case diagram.
- Document the main success scenario and an alternate flow for a key use case.

## 4. Deliverables

This lab demonstrates the application of requirements engineering and UML use-case modelling to a warehouse management scenario.
